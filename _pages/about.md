I am an **Assistant Professor in Embodied Intelligence** at **Beijing Zhongguancun Academy**, China. Before joining Beijing Zhongguancun Academy, I was a **Research Fellow** at the **University of Trento**, Italy, and previously a **Postdoctoral Researcher** in the Department of Cognitive Robotics at **Delft University of Technology (TU Delft)**, the Netherlands. Prior to that, I was an **Assistant Research Scientist** at the **Shenzhen Institute of Artificial Intelligence and Robotics for Society (AIRS)**, China. I received my PhD in **Mechatronic Engineering** from **Wuhan University**, China, and conducted part of my doctoral research at the **Italian Institute of Technology (IIT)**, Italy.

I serve as an **Associate Editor of IEEE Robotics and Automation Letters (RA-L)** and regularly review for leading robotics and control journals and conferences, including IJRR, IEEE T-RO, T-MECH, T-IE, T-ASE, RA-L, RSS, ICRA, IROS, and L4DC.

My research interests broadly lie in **robot learning, optimal control, and legged robotics**.
